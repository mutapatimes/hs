# Unit economics and growth scenarios

Working notes from a founder planning session (2026-08-25). All figures are estimates to be
revisited against real data; the churn and close-rate assumptions are guesses until Glen Norah
and early conversations give real signal.

## The market as identified

~900 potential clients worldwide, mixed multi-brand and single-brand.

| Segment | Share | Accounts | Profile | Tier | 100% penetration MRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | ~70% | 630 | Database often ≤1,000; mostly in-store sales; poor data capture | Discovery £150 | £94.5k |
| Mid | ~20% | 180 | 15k–75k databases | Signal £500 | £90k |
| Large | ~10% | 90 | 75k+, houses and groups | Atelier £1,200 | £108k |
| **Total TAM at current pricing** | | 900 | | | **£292.5k** |

Two structural facts:

1. **£80k MRR (the acquisition-ready target) is 27% of the entire identified market.** Outbound
   alone will not get there; a second motion is required (see below).
2. **The 630 small accounts cannot carry the business.** Even at impossible 100% conversion they
   are £94.5k, and they churn hardest (thin databases mean weak scan results mean weak retention).
   The 270 mid/large accounts alone hold £198k of MRR. The £80k goal fits entirely inside
   mid/large at ~40% penetration, with zero small clients.

## Unit economics per tier

COGS is near zero (one cloud service plus capped LLM spend, ~£200–400/mo total). The scarce
resource is founder hours per close.

| | Discovery | Signal | Atelier |
|---|---|---|---|
| MRR | £150 | £500 | £1,200 |
| Monthly churn (assumed) | 4% | 2% | 1.5% |
| Average lifetime | 25 mo | 50 mo | 67 mo |
| **Lifetime value** | **£3,750** | **£25,000** | **£80,000** |
| Effort per close | ~same call, same demo | ~same | maybe 2–3× |

One Signal ≈ 7 Discoveries in LTV for the same sales effort. One Atelier ≈ 21. Selling to smalls
with a founder-led motion pays a ~7× tax on founder time.

## Take-home targets, translated

UK Ltd, salary + dividends, roughly 30–35% total leakage at these levels.

| Take-home goal | Needed MRR | Example client mixes |
|---|---|---|
| £5k/mo | ~£7.5k | 15 Signal · or 10 Signal + 8 Discovery · or 6 Signal + 3 Atelier |
| £10k/mo | ~£17k | 26 Signal + 5 Atelier, or similar |
| Acquisition | £80k | ~45 Atelier + 54 Signal (50% / 30% of those segments), zero smalls needed |

£7.5k MRR = 15–20 closes from the 270 mid/large accounts (6–7% penetration). Reachable
founder-led in 6–9 months.

## Three 12-month scenarios (no paid acquisition)

**A. Volume-first (lead with smalls).** 8 small + 1 mid closes/mo. Month 12 ≈ £14k MRR across
~70 accounts; heaviest support load; 4% churn creates a replacement treadmill with a steady-state
ceiling around £42k (steady state ≈ new MRR / churn rate). **Can never reach £80k.**

**B. Balanced (what current pricing implies).** 3 mid + 3 small/mo, 1 Atelier per quarter.
Month 12 ≈ £20k MRR. £5k take-home month 5–6, £10k month 11–12. A third of founder time goes to
accounts worth 1/7th each.

**C. Upmarket-focused (recommended).** Founder time exclusively on the 270 mid/large; smalls are
self-serve only (free scan → self-upgrade, no calls, no onboarding). ~4 mid closes/mo + 1 Atelier
every 6 weeks + £300–600/mo of self-serve small drip. Month 12 ≈ £28–32k MRR from ~40 accounts.
£5k take-home ~month 4, £10k ~month 8–9. Fewer, larger, stickier clients — which is also what an
acquirer pays for (durable accounts, low support load, high NRR).

## Policy for the small 630

Cater ≠ sell. They can buy; the founder never sells.

- Keep Discovery at £150. A cheaper tier multiplies the accounts with the worst churn and data.
- Fully self-serve path: free scan → see the count → card → in. The ROI calculator and free scan
  are this funnel already.
- Annual at 2 months free (£1,500 up front) as the only concession: cash now, churn deferred,
  pricing stays simple. One line, not a new tier.
- Store Concierge (£14/mo second brand) stays parked until £10k+ MRR; before that it is a focus tax.

## The £80k path

The 900 list gets founder-led sales to £25–35k. Beyond that, compounding sources:

1. **Shopify public distribution** (in progress) — inbound beyond the 900. The single biggest lever.
2. **Net revenue retention** — seats at £15 (a 10-associate Signal client bills £575), multi-store,
   tier upgrades as databases grow. 110% NRR turns £35k into £80k in ~2.5 years with zero new
   logos; acquirers price NRR above nearly everything.
3. **Agencies / partners** — Shopify agencies and clienteling consultants resell to the long tail,
   so the small demographic is served without founder hours. (Shopify agencies build and run
   stores for merchants; they adopt tools across their whole client roster. One agency
   relationship can equal 10–30 small merchants. Typical terms: 20–30% recurring revenue share,
   or simply a better-together referral loop.)

Realistic timeline to £80k on scenario C plus the listing: 24–36 months.
