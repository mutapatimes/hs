# Halia for Japan: LINE

In Japan a boutique's clients are on LINE the way a London boutique's are on WhatsApp, and the
standard gesture at the till is "add our LINE". Halia works with that rather than around it.

## What works today

**The toolbar, where your team answers LINE.** Staff who reply to clients at
[chat.line.biz](https://chat.line.biz) (the LINE Official Account web chat) get the same Halia
toolbar the team has on WhatsApp Web: who this client is, the house templates, a selection to
send, a visit to book. LINE shows only a display name in chat, so Halia matches by name and says
so when it is unsure; the copied-client lookup covers the rest.

**Your pages, in Japanese.** Set the house voice's language to Japanese in Settings and every
page a client lands on follows: the selection page, the appointment invitation, the capture form.
Dates read 9月14日（土）15:00, yen prices carry no decimals, and the capture form takes the shape
Japanese forms take: family name first, a フリガナ field so your team addresses the client
correctly, the address postcode-first.

> The Japanese copy was drafted by Halia in polite keigo and is awaiting a native speaker's
> review. Have someone read the three pages before a Japanese client does.

**Sending through LINE.** The iPhone Share extension and Store Concierge both offer LINE beside
WhatsApp and email; the message opens in LINE's own share screen and your associate picks the
chat. Selection links pasted into LINE already arrive as image cards.

**Capture at the till.** Put your LINE Official Account's Basic ID (for example `@maison`) into
Settings and the capture tools grow an add-friend QR: the client scans it and your store's LINE
opens, one tap from adding you.

## Setting up a LINE Official Account

1. Create one free at [entry.line.biz](https://entry.line.biz) — self-serve, no approval needed
   to start.
2. Your Basic ID is shown in the LINE Official Account Manager under Settings; it looks like
   `@abc1234` until you buy a Premium ID with your own name.
3. Put the ID into Halia Settings under LINE.
4. Your team answers clients at [chat.line.biz](https://chat.line.biz) — install the Halia
   Chrome extension there and the toolbar appears.

Replying to clients in chat is free at any volume on LINE's free plan; only broadcast-style
messages count against a monthly allowance, and Halia sends none of those.
