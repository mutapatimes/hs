# Halia — App Store Screencast Script

Target length: 4:30 (inside Shopify's 3–8 min window).
Format: screen recording of the live app is primary; avatar (HeyGen) + voiceover narrates.
Data: record on a DEV STORE with fictional customers only. Never real client PII.

Shopify checklist this satisfies: onboarding flow ✓, merchant flow ✓, customer flow ✓,
main features ✓, no loud background noise ✓.

---

## SCENE 1 — What Halia is (0:00–0:20)
ON SCREEN: The Overview dashboard, already loaded, hero number visible (latent value + VIC count).
VOICEOVER:
"Halia is private client intelligence for luxury retail. It reads the customers you already
have in Shopify and surfaces the high-value clients hiding in your data, with the reason each
one matters. Here's how a merchant uses it, start to finish."

## SCENE 2 — Onboarding (0:20–1:05)
ON SCREEN: Shopify App Store listing page for Halia → click "Add app" → the install/permissions
screen → approve → the app opens embedded inside Shopify admin → the "Scoring your customers"
screen with its timer → it reloads by itself into the Overview.
VOICEOVER:
"Install is one click from the App Store. Halia uses Shopify's managed install: you approve
access to customers, orders and products, and it opens right inside your Shopify admin. On first
open it reads your order history and scores your customer base in the background. You see this
screen for a few seconds, then the Overview loads on its own. That is the whole setup."

## SCENE 3 — Overview: the headline (1:05–1:45)
ON SCREEN: Slowly pass over the Overview: total latent value, number of potential VICs, grade
mix, and the open-baskets strip.
VOICEOVER:
"The Overview answers one question: where is the untapped value. Here it's the total latent
value across surfaced clients, how many potential VICs there are, and how they grade. Down here
are open baskets: clients who started a purchase and stopped. These are the fastest conversations
to have today."

## SCENE 4 — Clients: the ranked list (1:45–2:20)
ON SCREEN: Click the Clients tab. Show the ranked list: name, grade, score, latent value. Use the
range filter, sort once.
VOICEOVER:
"The Clients view ranks everyone by opportunity, not just by past spend. Each row is a grade, a
score, and the latent value Halia estimates for that relationship. You can filter by time and
sort, but the default already puts the person you should call first at the top."

## SCENE 5 — The client drawer: the WHY (2:20–3:10)
ON SCREEN: Click a top client to open the drawer. Show: grade, the "Why this client surfaced"
reasons, the open basket contents, and the Message button.
VOICEOVER:
"This is the heart of Halia. Open a client and you see the grade, and underneath it, exactly why
they surfaced: the signals behind the score, in plain language. Here's an open basket worth over
a thousand pounds. Halia never asks you to trust a black box. It shows its reasons, then hands the
decision to you."

## SCENE 6 — Merchant acts: draft and send (3:10–3:55)
ON SCREEN: Click Message / "Draft with Halia" → a suggested message appears in the house voice →
edit one word → send it out through the clienteling toolbar on WhatsApp (or copy to send). Then
show the client moving along the Pipeline board.
VOICEOVER:
"When you're ready to reach out, Halia drafts the message in your brand's voice. You edit it or
send as is, on the channel you already use. The advisor stays in control of every word. As you
work, clients move through a simple outreach pipeline, so nothing gets forgotten."

## SCENE 7 — Customer flow: what the client receives (3:55–4:20)
ON SCREEN: Show the receiving side: the client gets the personal message, and opens a branded
catalogue / lookbook link (the app-proxy /a/catalogue page on the store's own domain).
VOICEOVER:
"On the client's side, it feels personal, because it is. They receive a message from their
advisor, and can open a branded catalogue on the store's own domain, chosen for them. No app to
download, nothing to sign up for."

## SCENE 8 — Zero-retention + close (4:20–4:35)
ON SCREEN: Back to Overview; optionally the Settings/privacy line.
VOICEOVER:
"And Halia holds nothing. Scoring happens against your live Shopify data and is not retained
after the session. That's Halia: it finds the clients worth your attention, tells you why, and
lets you do what only you can do, which is have the conversation."

## Recording notes
- Resolution: record at 1920×1080, app zoomed so text is legible.
- Mouse: move deliberately, pause ~1s on each number before narrating it.
- Audio: quiet room, or use the HeyGen/ElevenLabs voice track only (no room tone). Shopify
  explicitly penalises loud background noise.
- No Shopify logo close-ups needed; showing the app embedded in admin is expected and fine here
  (the logo rule is for static listing images, not the demo video).
- Upload to YouTube (Unlisted) or Vimeo, paste that link in the Screencast URL field.

## SCENE 8 — Billing, on Shopify (add before the close)
ON SCREEN: Settings → Billing. All plans visible: Free scan, Discovery, Signal, Atelier, Maison.
Click "Choose Signal" → Shopify's subscription approval page opens → Approve (test charge) →
the app reloads with Signal marked "Current plan". Then scroll to Maison and show "Talk to us".
VOICEOVER:
"Plans are billed through Shopify. Every plan is listed here. Choosing one takes you to
Shopify's own approval page, and you land back in the app on the new plan. Maison is custom
pricing for groups and the largest houses; that one starts with a conversation. Switching to
Free cancels through Shopify, from the same screen."
