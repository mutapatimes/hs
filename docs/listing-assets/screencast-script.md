# Halia: App Store screencast script

Length: about 7 minutes (Shopify allows 3 to 8). English voiceover plus on-screen captions for
every step, so it reads without sound.

Shopify's note on the last video (2026-10-14): it "doesn't provide enough context on how to
properly use the app and was only stuck on a specific admin section". This version walks the
whole setup from the install screen, one feature at a time, and every scene opens with a
caption naming the step.

**Record on the Maison Aurelle development store**: fictional clients only, never real ones.
Uninstall Halia from it first, so the recording starts from a genuine install. Keep
`HALIA_SHOPIFY_BILLING_TEST=true` so plan changes are test charges.

Captions: white text on a dark strip at the bottom, one line, shown for the whole scene.

---

## 1. Install (0:00 to 0:45)
CAPTION: Step 1: Install Halia
ON SCREEN: Shopify admin → Apps → Halia's install page → "Install" → the permissions screen
(customers, orders, products) → approve.
VOICEOVER: "Halia finds the high-value clients already in your Shopify customers. Install it from
the App Store. Shopify asks you to approve access to customers, orders and products. Approve,
and Halia opens inside your Shopify admin."

## 2. First scoring (0:45 to 1:20)
CAPTION: Step 2: Halia scores your customers, no setup needed
ON SCREEN: the "Scoring your customers" screen with its progress → it reloads by itself into
the Overview. Do not cut this wait short; show that it finishes on its own.
VOICEOVER: "On first open Halia reads your order history and grades every customer. There is
nothing to configure. When scoring finishes, the Overview opens by itself."

## 3. Overview (1:20 to 2:00)
CAPTION: Step 3: The Overview, where the value is
ON SCREEN: hover each figure in turn: latent value, potential VICs, average spend, the grade
mix (A*, A, B), the plays (Gone quiet, Fresh), open baskets.
VOICEOVER: "The Overview shows how much value sits in clients you already have, how many
potential VICs Halia found, and how they grade. A star is strongest. The plays group clients by
what to do next: Gone quiet are good clients who have not ordered lately, Fresh are new clients
worth a welcome."

## 4. Clients and the reasons (2:00 to 2:50)
CAPTION: Step 4: Open a client to see why they surfaced
ON SCREEN: left nav → Clients → sort by grade → open Lady Ottoline Ferrers-Vane → the drawer:
grade, the reasons list, order history.
VOICEOVER: "Clients lists everyone Halia graded, best first. Open one and you see why they
surfaced, in plain facts from their own orders and account: here, a prime London address, a
private office, an assistant who places the orders. Halia never guesses in the dark; it shows
its reasons."

## 5. Reach out (2:50 to 3:40)
CAPTION: Step 5: Message a client and track it
ON SCREEN: in the drawer → Message → pick a template → the first name fills in → copy or open
in email → "Mark as contacted" → left nav → Pipeline → the client now in "Contacted".
VOICEOVER: "From the client, choose a message. Halia fills in their name and your sign-off;
send it from your own email or WhatsApp. Mark them as contacted and they move along the
Pipeline, so the team can see who has been reached."

## 6. Campaigns and catalogues (3:40 to 4:30)
CAPTION: Step 6: Target a group, and share a private selection
ON SCREEN: Campaigns → New campaign → target grades A* and A → save → the campaign card with
its members. Then Catalogues → New → pick three products → copy the link → open it: a branded
page on the store's own domain.
VOICEOVER: "Campaigns target a group of clients by grade or signal and track the sales that
follow. Catalogues build a private selection of your products, shared as a link on your own
store's domain."

## 7. Settings (4:30 to 5:10)
CAPTION: Step 7: Settings: alerts, templates and your team
ON SCREEN: Settings → Alerts (turn on a top-grade order alert) → Templates (edit one line, save)
→ Team (add a seat for an associate).
VOICEOVER: "In Settings you choose alerts for when a top client orders, edit your message
templates, and add your team. Each associate gets their own seat for the iPhone app and the
Chrome extension."

## 8. Plans: upgrade, switch, downgrade (5:10 to 6:40)
CAPTION: Step 8: Change plan through Shopify, up or down, any time
ON SCREEN, in one take:
1. Settings → Billing. Show the four plans: Free scan, Discovery, Signal, Atelier.
2. "Choose Discovery" → Shopify's approval page → Approve (test charge) → back in Halia with
   Discovery marked "Current plan".
3. "Switch to Signal" → Shopify's approval page → Approve → back in Halia on Signal.
4. "Switch to Free" → confirm → "Plan cancelled" → Free marked "Current plan".
5. Shopify admin → Settings → Billing → Charges: show the Halia charges listed.
VOICEOVER: "Plans are billed by Shopify. Choose a plan and Shopify shows its approval page; you
come straight back to Halia on the new plan. Switching to another plan works the same way and
replaces the old one immediately. Switching to Free cancels the subscription through Shopify,
from the same screen, with no need to contact us. Every charge appears in your Shopify billing."

## 9. Close (6:40 to 7:00)
CAPTION: Halia: the clients worth your attention, and why
ON SCREEN: back on the Overview.
VOICEOVER: "That is Halia: install, let it score, open a client, reach out. Your clients'
details stay in Shopify; Halia keeps no copy of them."

- Quiet room, or a generated voice track only.
