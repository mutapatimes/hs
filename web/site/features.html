Everything Halia does

In the order you meet it: connect your store, see who quietly matters, act on it where your team already works, and let the rest run in the background. Halia keeps no copy of your customers, it reads your own store live and forgets.

A few minutes, once

Nothing here needs a developer.

1

Connect your store

One click, read-only, revocable any time

Halia reads your orders and customers to score them. Shopify installs in one click; WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Centra and SCAYLE connect with a key. It can only read, never write or charge, and you can disconnect whenever you like.

2

Tell Halia what your house offers

A short questionnaire, in your trade's own words

Seven quick questions that branch on what you sell: a jeweller is asked about polishing, resizing and valuation, a fashion house about alterations and monogramming. You mark each service complimentary, complimentary for a top client, or chargeable, and pick a house voice. This is what lets Halia offer the right things and never promise something you charge for. Every question is skippable, and you can revise it any time in Settings.

3

Set your preferences

Threshold, sign-off, benchmarks, message templates

Choose the spend level that separates a known VIP from a hidden one, your sign-off name, your own value benchmarks, and edit the 28 ready-made outreach templates that ship with the account.

The dashboard

Halia scores your whole book and surfaces the people worth your attention.

4

Overview

One screen: what deserves your attention today

The headline numbers, the clients who have gone quiet, the baskets left open right now, how value is spread across grades, where your clients are, and the strongest signals firing across the book. Charts appear only where there is something to show.

5

The client list

Every surfaced client, filtered and sorted your way

Filter by grade, city, signal or recency, sort by score, value or last order, and download the lot as a spreadsheet. Each client is one click from their full profile.

6

Ask in plain EnglishAI

Describe who you're after, Halia sets the filters

Type "hidden A-tier clients in London who haven't been contacted" and the list filters itself. Halia never invents a client: it only chooses from filters the page already has, so the worst case is a view that finds nothing.

7

The client profile

Who they are, why, and what to do next

Their grade and score, and why they surfaced laid out as evidence you can check: a work email at a firm that pays, a prime address, deliveries to notable places. A one-line read of what makes them matter, their order history, and a place to log a "good call" that teaches Halia your book. Enlarge it for the full record.

8

Where they are

Your clients on a map

See how your best clients cluster by area, so a trunk show, a pop-up or a private evening lands where the value already is.

9

Orders & open baskets

Recent orders, and the checkouts left unpaid

The latest orders at a glance, and the baskets a client reached but did not pay for, so a quiet, timely note can recover them while they are still warm.

10

This weekAI

A Monday briefing, in a few lines

What deserves attention this week, counted from your own book: top clients who ordered, proven clients gone quiet, the money sitting in open baskets. Every figure is real; the AI only phrases them.

Reach the client, everywhere you work

11

Campaigns

Target a moment, then watch it work

Build a campaign around a grade or a signal, then monitor the sales and the reactivations it drives, with links that attribute the purchases back to it.

12

Catalogues

A shareable selection, on your own domain

Curate your products into a catalogue, style it to your brand, and share it as a web page or a print-ready PDF from your own storefront address, personalised to the client it is sent to.

13

The Concierge

A companion that follows your team

A slim panel that rides alongside your store admin, WhatsApp, Gmail and more. The moment a client appears on screen it tells your associate who they are, their grade, why, and the next move. No copy of the customer is kept: it reads the person on the page and asks your account live.

14

The briefAI

Read this conversation, and know what to do

One press turns the conversation on screen into three things: where the relationship stands, the next moves worth making (each a button that carries them out), and a reply ready to send, written for this person, in your house voice, offering only what you actually provide. If a written brief is ever unavailable, it falls back to your own book and templates, so the button is never dead.

15

Suggest, cart & bespoke catalogueAI

Put the right pieces in front of them, in seconds

Halia suggests a handful of pieces for the client, each with a reason your associate can say out loud, drawn from what they have bought and what they just asked for. Add the ones you like, then send them as a ready-to-pay cart or a bespoke catalogue addressed to the client. It only ever suggests products that are genuinely in your store.

16

Templates, where you type

The right message, one click into the reply box

Your outreach templates, searchable and filled with the client's name, dropped straight into WhatsApp or Gmail. Cancellations, exchanges, gifting, appointments, win-backs, and more than two dozen scenarios besides.

17

Pipeline & outreach log

So the whole team stays in step

Add a client to your outreach list or a running campaign in one click, and log that you contacted them, with a reason, so nobody messages the same client twice. It writes into your own store, never into Halia.

18

Team mode

Keep everyone looking after the same clients

Inside Slack or Teams the panel turns to your team: a shared contact log, a to-do list built from your book, and messages ready to paste, so a small team can clientele like a large one.

19

Everywhere your team already works

Store admin, WhatsApp, Gmail, Instagram, Calendar, helpdesks

The same intelligence appears in your store admin, WhatsApp Web, Gmail, Instagram DMs and Google Calendar, and in the support and marketing tools your team uses: Gorgias, Zendesk and Klaviyo. Support sees when a ticket is from an A* client; a marketer sees who quietly matters while building a flow.

20

24

We keep none of your data

Halia keeps no copy of your customers

This is the difference, not a footnote. Halia reads your own store in the moment, works out who matters, shows your team, and forgets, in minutes. There is no customer database to breach, and switching Halia off leaves nothing to delete. The only durable record is your own settings and your encrypted store connection.

25

Plans & billing

Priced to the value, not the volume

Flat tiers by the size of your book. The recent-window view is open; the full history and the deeper features are the upgrade.

A note on the AI. The features marked AI write in words: the brief, the drafted reply, product suggestions, the plain-English search and the weekly briefing. They are an optional layer. Without them, every one of those features still works from your scored book and your own templates, so nothing is ever a dead button. And nothing Halia writes is ever sent on its own, an associate confirms every message.
