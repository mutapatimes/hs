Halia

Show a client's grade, the reasons behind it, and the next move right where you already work: your store admin, WhatsApp Web and Gmail. The extension reads the client on the page and asks your Halia account. It stores nothing.

Connect

Open Halia and it will find this extension. In Settings, press Connect this browser and you are in. Installed through Shopify? Open Halia from your Shopify admin's Apps menu instead; the same button is in its Settings.

▸ Sign in with a token instead
In Halia, open Settings → Browser extension and generate a token. It is shown once. Paste it here.

Your store website

Add your store's address and Halia rides along on your own site: open any product, collection, care or returns page and send it to the right client with a note that fits the page. WooCommerce stores also get the full panel inside wp-admin. You'll be asked to grant access to that one site.

    Halia on any page

    Highlight a name, email or phone number on any page for a one-tap look-up, and drop a house template into any message box, filled for the client you pick. This needs permission to read the pages you use it on, so it stays off until you turn it on here.

    Getting started

    1. Open a customer or order in your store admin, a chat in WhatsApp Web, or an email in Gmail.
    2. The Halia card appears in the corner with their grade, why, and the next move.
    3. Pick a message template and copy it, or jump to the client in Halia.
    4. Browsing your own store? Open the Share tab, choose a client from your book, pick an opener that fits the page, and message them the link.
